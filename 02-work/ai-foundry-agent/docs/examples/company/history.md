# Company History

## Founding
Our company was founded on **January 10, 2020** by John Doe and Jane Smith in San Francisco, California. The initial idea came from recognizing a gap in the market for intelligent knowledge management systems.

## Early Days (2020)
- **January 10, 2020**: Company incorporation completed
- **February 1, 2020**: Hired first 3 employees
- **March 15, 2020**: Started product development
- **June 1, 2020**: Completed MVP (Minimum Viable Product)
- **July 20, 2020**: Launched private beta with 10 companies
