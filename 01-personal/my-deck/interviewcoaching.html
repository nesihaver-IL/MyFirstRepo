What happens during a Coacher Interview

4

Detailed feedback on strengths and weakness areas. Specific techniques to improve weak answers. Action plan for continued practice before the real interview.

The STAR Method Framework

Structuring compelling interview stories

Situation

Context: Set the scene. What was the challenge or project? When? Where? Who was involved? Give enough detail so the interviewer understands the context without getting lost in irrelevant details.

Task

Your role: What was YOUR specific responsibility? What did you need to accomplish? Be clear about your individual contribution, not just what the team did.

Action

What you did: The steps YOU took. The decisions YOU made. The problems YOU solved. This is the most important part — show your initiative, creativity, and problem-solving.

Result

What happened: Quantifiable outcomes when possible (increased sales by 20%, reduced turnaround time by 30%, improved customer satisfaction from 75% to 95%). Focus on the impact of YOUR actions. Show learning and growth from the experience.

Identifying Your Strengths

What makes you stand out

During Mock Interview

  • ▸ Clear communication style (articulate, concise, confident)
  • ▸ Strong storytelling ability (engaging narratives with clear structure)
  • ▸ Leadership & initiative (taking charge, driving results)
  • ▸ Problem-solving skills (creative solutions, analytical thinking)
  • ▸ Collaboration & teamwork (listening, supporting others)

Coach identifies these during the mock interview and helps you emphasize them in your responses.

Identifying & Improving Weakness Areas

Targeted feedback for improvement

Common Weakness Areas

  • ▸ Rambling, unclear answers
  • ▸ Nervous filler words ("um," "like," "you know")
  • ▸ Not showcasing personal contribution
  • ▸ Weak closing without impact statement
  • ▸ Not connecting answer to job requirements

Specific Improvements

  • ▸ Structure with STAR method
  • ▸ Practice eliminating filler words
  • ▸ Emphasize "I" not "we" appropriately
  • ▸ End with clear result/impact
  • ▸ Link to job posting requirements
