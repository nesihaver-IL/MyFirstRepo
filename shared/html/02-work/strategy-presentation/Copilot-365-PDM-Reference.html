UC7: Teams 365 — Your Daily Command Center

Teams is where decisions live. Copilot helps you capture, organize, and reference them—searchable forever.

Enhancement 1: Facilitator — Meeting to Minutes

💡 Start Facilitator at meeting open. It auto-tracks agenda, captures decisions in real-time, and generates structured minutes post-meeting.

How it works:

  • During meeting: Facilitator displays agenda, participants see live notes on screen
  • Post-meeting: Copilot extracts decisions with owners and flags risks
  • Output: Recap tab in Teams with full transcript and action items
From this Teams meeting transcript, extract: 1. **Minutes**: 2-3 sentence summary of discussion 2. **Decisions Made**: What was decided? (with owner + confidence) 3. **Assumptions**: What did we assume to be true? 4. **Risks Identified**: What could go wrong? (impact, owner) 5. **Action Items**: Specific tasks (owner, due date, context) 6. **Open Questions**: What still needs clarification? Format: Concise, fact-based, no interpretation. If not discussed, omit.
Pro Tip: Use the "Follow Up" button during meeting to auto-create tasks with meeting context. No manual entry needed.

Enhancement 2: Decision Logging in Channels

📌 Pin meeting recap to dedicated decision channel. Creates searchable, accountable decision record.

Workflow:

  1. Meeting ends → Facilitator recap appears
  2. Ask Copilot to create decision card (see prompt below)
  3. Pin decision card to #threat-assessments or #decisions channel
  4. Result: Find any decision in 10 seconds, 6 months later
Create a decision card for this meeting: **Decision:** [What was decided?] **Owner:** [Who owns implementation?] **Deadline:** [When?] **Rationale:** [Why this approach? What were alternatives?] **Risks Flagged:** [Any concerns raised?] **Dependencies:** [What else needs to happen first?] **Success Criteria:** [How do we know we succeeded?]

Enhancement 3: Quick Chat Reactions & Threading

✅ Use emoji reactions for approvals (✅/❌/❓) and threads to keep conversations organized. Reduces notification spam.

Best Practices:

  • ✅ = Approved, move forward
  • ❌ = Concerns, needs discussion
  • ❓ = Review needed, unclear
  • ⏰ = Urgent, needs immediate attention
Workflow Hack: Reply in thread to avoid @mention spam. React with emoji for quick decisions that don't need thread. Default to threads for security-sensitive topics.

UC5: Outlook — Email Intelligence

Email is still critical for compliance and decision trails. Copilot helps you find and act on important messages faster.

Enhancement 1: Prioritized Inbox

🎯 Let Copilot auto-sort inbox by importance. VIP senders and critical keywords float to top. Runs overnight.

Setup (3 minutes):

Settings → Copilot → Prioritize Inbox → On Add your priorities: - VIP Senders: [manager, security lead, compliance officer] - Keywords: "threat", "critical", "approval", "blocker", "deadline" - External Domain: [key partners/vendors] - Exclude: newsletters, system alerts, low-priority notifications
Pro Tip: Let it learn for 3-5 days, then refine based on what it got wrong. Copilot gets better with feedback.

Enhancement 2: Copilot Chat in Outlook

💬 Ask Copilot questions about email threads without leaving Outlook. Summarize, extract action items, draft replies.

Common Prompts:

"Summarize my emails about [threat/project] from [timeframe]. What decisions were made? Who owns what?" "Extract action items from this email thread. Who is responsible for each item? What are the deadlines?" "Draft a reply to [sender] covering [points]. Keep tone [formal/collaborative/urgent]." "What's the status of [initiative]? Has [person] responded to the last email?"

Enhancement 3: Schedule Meeting from Email

📅 Select email thread → ask Copilot to schedule follow-up. Auto-drafts agenda, finds time, creates invite.

Example: Instead of 10 minutes of scheduling back-and-forth, Copilot reads the email thread and creates a meeting invite in 30 seconds.

When to use: Escalations, unresolved discussions, decisions that need live debate. Perfect for async-to-sync handoff.

UC3: Excel — Risk & Project Scoring

Turn threat trackers and project plans into smart spreadsheets with AI-driven scoring and forecasting.

Enhancement 1: Agent Mode — Calculated Columns

🧮 Add risk/priority scores automatically. Copilot writes formulas based on existing data (severity, exploitability, impact).

Example: Threat tracker with CVSS score, exploitability, business impact → Copilot creates "Risk Level" column with formula.

Add a 'Risk Level' column (1-5 scale) based on: - Severity (CVSS or similar) - Exploitability (how likely to be used) - Business Impact (what breaks if this happens) Show me the formula you used. Weight severity 40%, exploitability 30%, impact 30%.
Pro Tip: Start with clean data (well-structured rows/columns). Ask Copilot to "Show insights" after scoring to find patterns.

Enhancement 2: Show Insights

🔍 Let Copilot auto-detect patterns you might miss. Identifies outliers, trends, and anomalies.
Analyze this threat tracker and identify: 1. Top 3 threats by risk score 2. Any trends (increasing, seasonal, related threats) 3. Outliers or anomalies worth investigating 4. Remediation gaps (high-risk items not yet resolved) 5. Themes (e.g., "supply chain threats", "authentication risks")

Enhancement 3: Forecast Timeline

📈 AI detects velocity patterns and predicts when you'll complete high-risk items. Flags timeline risks early.
Based on historical remediation times, forecast: 1. When we'll complete all high-risk items 2. Which items are at risk of missing deadline 3. Velocity trend (getting faster or slower?) 4. Bottlenecks (resources, complexity, dependencies) 5. Recommended remediation order (critical path)

UC6: Teams Meeting Intelligence

Copilot captures context during meetings and extracts actionable insights after. Two key moments: during and after.

During Meeting — Real-Time Capture

🎤 Ask Copilot mid-meeting to summarize discussion, identify disagreements, and flag open questions.
"Summarize the main discussion points so far. Where do we agree? Where do we disagree? What are the unresolved questions?" "Who has been assigned what? Summarize action items captured so far." "What are the key risks or concerns raised?"

After Meeting — Structured Extraction (⭐ KEY)

📋 Post-meeting: Run extraction prompt to get minutes, decisions, assumptions, risks, and action items. Async team members catch up in 2 minutes.
**DETAILED EXTRACTION PROMPT:** From this Teams meeting transcript, extract: 1. **Minutes**: 2-3 sentence summary of discussion 2. **Decisions Made**: What was decided? (with owner + confidence level) 3. **Assumptions**: What did we assume to be true? 4. **Risks Identified**: What could go wrong? (severity, impact) 5. **Action Items**: Specific tasks (owner, due date, context) 6. **Open Questions**: What still needs clarification? 7. **Dependencies**: What needs to happen first? Format: Concise, fact-based, no interpretation/bias.

Follow-Up Extraction (for Async Team):

**FOLLOW-UP PROMPT FOR TEAM MEMBERS WHO MISSED MEETING:** If I missed this meeting, tell me: 1. Top 3 things I need to know 2. Any decisions affecting my work specifically 3. What am I responsible for (if anything)? 4. When do I need to act? 5. Who should I follow up with?
Workflow: After meeting, pin the extraction to the Teams channel immediately. Non-attendees can catch up in 2 minutes. Decisions are logged before people forget context.

Recap by Speakers

How to use: Open Recap tab → scroll to "By Speakers" section → reference who owned each topic in follow-ups.

Known Activity (research areas, affiliations) 5. Contact Pathways (how to reach them) 6. Confidence Level for each finding Source everything. Flag what's public vs. internal.

📊 Analyze competitor approaches, market positioning, and gaps. Informs threat modeling and strategic decisions.

Strategic Partnerships 6. SWOT vs. Our Approach 7. What We Should Learn Source everything. Flag confidence levels.

Best Practices & Tips

For Teams & Meetings

  • Always use Facilitator. Start at meeting open (not mid-meeting) for best agenda context.
  • Pin decisions immediately. Do it while meeting context is fresh. 6 months later, you'll thank yourself.
  • Use threads for async discussions. Reduce @mention spam. Reserve threads for security-sensitive topics.
  • Tag important speakers. During meeting, Copilot will highlight their contributions in recap.

For Outlook

  • Let Prioritized Inbox learn. First 3-5 days, it'll make mistakes. Refine based on feedback.
  • Use Copilot Chat for context. Before replying to a long thread, ask Copilot to summarize. Saves misunderstandings.
  • Customize aggressively. Add your own keywords (e.g., "escalation", "compliance", "threat").

For Excel

  • Start with clean data. Well-structured rows/columns. Copilot works best with consistent input.
  • Always review formulas. Copilot-generated formulas are readable. Check the logic before you trust it.
  • Use Show Insights. Let Copilot find patterns you might miss. Great for anomaly detection.

For Researcher

  • Save working prompts to library. Reuse for consistency. Team gets same research quality.
  • Ask for citations. Always include "Source everything" in research prompts. Compliance requirement.
  • Combine with Excel. Research data → Excel for scoring/ranking. Researcher + Excel = powerful combo.

FAQ

Q: Does Copilot work offline?

A: No. Copilot requires Microsoft 365 + Copilot license + internet connection. All features are cloud-based.

Q: What's the difference between Facilitator and regular meeting notes?

A: Facilitator auto-structures notes, tracks agenda, flags decisions, and creates action items. Regular notes are just text. Facilitator adds AI context.

Q: Can I train Copilot on my company's data?

A: Yes, via Copilot Studio (enterprise feature). You can build custom agents that know your systems, terminology, workflows.

Q: What if Copilot extracts a decision incorrectly?

A: Edit the extraction. Copilot gives you the first draft. You're still in control. Review before you pin decisions to channels.

Q: How much does Copilot cost?

A: Usually $30/user/month add-on to Microsoft 365 subscription. Check with your IT/procurement team.

Q: Can I use this on mobile?

A: Facilitator has mobile version. Outlook, Teams, Excel have mobile Copilot access. Not all features on mobile (e.g., Agent Mode in Excel is web-only).
