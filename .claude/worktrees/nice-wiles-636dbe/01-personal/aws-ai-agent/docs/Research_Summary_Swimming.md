## 3. Performance Metrics & KPIs
* **The SR-SL Trade-off:** Efficiency is the balance between **Stroke Rate (SR)** and **Stroke Length (SL)**. For masters, maintaining SL is the priority to avoid "fighting the water."
* **Sprint KPIs:** Starts and turns can contribute up to **25%** of total time in 50m-100m events.
* **Long-Distance KPIs:** Success is driven by **Free-Swimming Speed (FSS)** and consistent turn efficiency.
* **Predictive Modeling:** Using variables like FSS and turn approach (in5), race times can be predicted within a 0.1s bias.
