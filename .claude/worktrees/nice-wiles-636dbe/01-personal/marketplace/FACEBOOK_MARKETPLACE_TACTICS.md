## 📱 Step-by-Step: Posting Your Items

### BEFORE You Post:

**✅ Prepare Your Photos**
```
Photo 1: Main product shot (well-lit, clean background)
Photo 2: Different angle / side view
Photo 3: Close-up of brand/model label
Photo 4: Any defects or wear (builds trust!)
Photo 5: Size reference (measuring tape or comparison)
Photo 6: Product in use / styled shot
Photo 7-10: Additional details, accessories, packaging
```

**Best Photo Settings:**
- Natural daylight (10 AM - 3 PM near window)
- No flash - causes glare and shadows
- Clean white/neutral background
- Hold phone horizontally (landscape)
- Multiple angles required

**✅ Get Your Profile Ready**
1. Profile photo uploaded (verified sellers get 2x more messages)
2. Verify your identity (Settings → Account → Identity Confirmation)
3. Link payment method if offering shipping
4. Join 5-10 local buy/sell groups

## 🚀 Posting Strategy (Maximize Visibility)

### Optimal Posting Times:
- **Weekdays**: 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM (after work hours)
- **Weekends**: 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM (morning browsers)
- **Avoid**: Monday mornings, late nights (11 PM+)

### Step-by-Step Posting:

**1. Create Your Listing**
- Navigate to Marketplace → Create New Listing
- Select "Item for Sale"

**2. Category Selection (Critical for Search)**
- **TAG Watch**: Jewelry & Accessories → Watches
- **Coffee Machine**: Home & Garden → Kitchen & Dining
- **Bed**: Home & Garden → Furniture → Bedroom
- **Furniture**: Home & Garden → Furniture → [specific type]

**3. Title Formula (50 characters max)**
```
[Brand] [Model] - [Condition] - [Key Feature]

Examples:
✅ "TAG Heuer Watch - Like New - Original Box"
✅ "Queen Bed Frame - Solid Wood - Modern"
✅ "Cuisinart Coffee Maker - 14 Cup - Perfect"
❌ "Nice watch for sale"
❌ "Bed"
```

**4. Price Strategy**
- Use charm pricing: $299, $149, $79 (not round numbers)
- 10-15% below market = sells in 48 hours
- Too low = people suspicious
- Include original price in description to show value

**5. Location Settings**
- Set accurate pickup location (helps with local search)
- Expand delivery radius if offering delivery
- Enable "Shipping" only for small valuable items (watch)

**6. Description (Use Template Below)**

## 🔥 ADVANCED TACTICS FOR FAST SALES

### 1. **The "Share to Groups" Strategy**
**Post in this order:**
1. Main Facebook Marketplace (first)
2. Wait 5 minutes
3. Share to local buy/sell groups (3-5 groups)
4. Share to specific interest groups:
   - Watch collectors (for TAG watch)
   - Home decor groups (for furniture)
   - Moving sale groups (for everything)

### 2. **The "Bump" Strategy**
After 24 hours with no sale:
- Comment on your own listing: "Still available!"
- This brings it back to top of feed
- Do this every 24 hours
- DO NOT delete and repost (gets flagged as spam)

### 3. **The "Price Drop" Strategy**
If no bites after 3 days:
- Edit listing and reduce price by $10-20
- Facebook notifies everyone who saved your listing!
- Creates urgency: "Price just dropped!"

### 4. **The "Bundle Deal" Strategy**
Create separate post:
```
"BUNDLE DEAL! Buy any 2 items, get 15% off total!
• TAG Watch
• Coffee Maker
• Bed Frame
• [Furniture Item]

Message for combo pricing! Moving sale - need gone this week!"
```

### 5. **The "Cross-Promote" Strategy**
On your personal Facebook (not Marketplace):
```
"🚨 MOVING SALE 🚨

Selling quality items - friends & family discount available!

• TAG Watch - $[price]
• Coffee Maker - $[price]
• Queen Bed - $[price]
• [Furniture] - $[price]

```

## 🚨 RED FLAGS - Avoid These Buyers

**DO NOT engage with:**
- Asks for your email/phone before meeting
- Wants to send check or money order
- Asks you to ship internationally
- Sends "verification code" requests
- Offers MORE than asking price
- Wants to use unknown payment apps
- Can't meet in person (for local pickup items)

**Safe Transaction Rules:**
1. Meet in PUBLIC place (police station parking lot is best)
2. Bring a friend
3. Cash is safest (or instant payment apps)
4. Don't go to their house alone
5. Don't invite them to your house

## 📊 If Not Selling - Troubleshooting

### Not Getting Views?
- ❌ Poor photos → Retake in natural light
- ❌ Wrong category → Change category
- ❌ Generic title → Add specific brand/model
- ❌ Posted at bad time → Repost 6-8 PM

### Getting Views But No Messages?
- ❌ Price too high → Drop 15-20%
- ❌ Description unclear → Add more details
- ❌ Missing info → Add dimensions, condition details
- ❌ No value shown → Add original price, savings

### Getting Messages But No Sales?
- ❌ Slow responses → Reply within 15 min
- ❌ Inflexible location → Offer delivery
- ❌ Unclear meeting process → Suggest specific places/times
- ❌ Poor communication → Be friendly, professional
