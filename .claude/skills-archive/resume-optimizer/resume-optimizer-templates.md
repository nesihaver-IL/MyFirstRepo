# Resume Optimizer — Templates & Examples

This file contains reusable templates and examples for the `resume-optimizer` skill.

## Template 1: Weak-to-Strong Bullet Rewrite Examples

### Weak Bullets (Common Anti-Patterns)

```
❌ Responsible for managing product roadmap and working with stakeholders
❌ Helped the team improve code quality through code reviews
❌ Assisted in the migration of legacy systems to microservices
❌ Worked on improving customer retention by implementing new features
❌ Collaborated with marketing to plan campaign strategy
```

### Strong Bullets (Using Achievement Framework)

```
✅ Led product roadmap prioritization across 3 engineering teams and 5 executive stakeholders,
   aligning 12-month strategic plan with company OKRs

✅ Established mandatory code review standards and peer review culture, reducing production
   incidents by 35% within 6 months

✅ Architected and executed migration of 200+ microservices from monolith to Kubernetes,
   reducing infrastructure costs by 40% and improving deployment speed from monthly to hourly

✅ Drove feature prioritization for retention, delivering 3 new features that improved 30-day
   retention rate from 62% to 78% in one quarter

✅ Negotiated marketing campaign strategy with 8-person cross-functional team, securing executive
   approval and $500K annual budget for growth initiatives
```

## Template 3: Achievement Rewrite Framework

### The Formula
```
[STRONG VERB] + [SCOPE/CONTEXT] + [OUTCOME/METRIC]
```

### Strong Verbs by Category

**Leadership & Direction:**
- Led, Drove, Orchestrated, Spearheaded, Championed, Established, Pioneered

**Building & Creation:**
- Built, Designed, Architected, Engineered, Developed, Launched, Implemented, Created

**Improvement & Optimization:**
- Optimized, Improved, Enhanced, Accelerated, Streamlined, Refined, Transformed, Scaled

**Problem Solving:**
- Resolved, Fixed, Diagnosed, Solved, Addressed, Eliminated, Uncovered, Identified

**Collaboration & Influence:**
- Collaborated, Negotiated, Partnered, Aligned, Influenced, Persuaded, Facilitated, Mentored

**Data & Analysis:**
- Analyzed, Measured, Demonstrated, Quantified, Discovered, Tracked, Monitored, Validated

**Reduction & Cost Savings:**
- Reduced, Decreased, Cut, Lowered, Minimized, Eliminated, Avoided, Saved

**Paragraph 1 — Positioning (2-3 sentences)**
```
[Seniority + specialty]. [Years of experience] building [outcome type] at
[company stage / industry]. Focused on [key problem or value delivered].
```

**Paragraph 2 — Track Record (2-3 sentences)**

## Best Practices Summary

### ✅ DO:
- Use specific metrics from your background (don't invent)
- Focus on outcomes your candidates can prove
- Tailor language to the market benchmark (use their terminology naturally)
- Keep bullets scannable (2-3 lines max)
- Show progression (early career: execution, mid-career: leadership, senior: strategy)
- Use strong verbs appropriate to the role
- Lead with impact, not process
- Test for ATS readability (no graphics, clean formatting)

### ❌ DON'T:
- Invent metrics or achievements
- Use clichés ("team player", "hard worker", "go-getter")
- Bury the lead (put outcome first, not "responsible for")
- Keyword-stuff (it looks inauthentic and hurts ATS)
- Include irrelevant experience (trim ruthlessly)
- Use weak verbs (helped, assisted, participated)
- Make claims you can't defend in an interview
- Use unusual formatting (ATS can't parse it)
