Choose the Right Tool
Each tool shines in different scenarios — use the best one for the job
🤖 ChatGPT
Best for general tasks, building custom GPT bots for your team, rapid data analysis, and maximum day-to-day versatility.
✨ Gemini
Best for Google Workspace users, deep research reports, and handling very long documents or multimodal inputs.
🍌 Nano Banana
Best for creating professional visuals, consistent brand imagery, marketing assets, and iterative creative design.
🔮 Claude Chat
Best for deep analysis, long documents, live Artifacts, complex reasoning, and safety-sensitive conversations.
📔 NotebookLM
Best for research, legal review, team briefings, and any workflow where accuracy and source citations are critical.
